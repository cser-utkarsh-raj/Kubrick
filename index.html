THE ENGINE

Objective first.
Human always.

Kubrick is built for spoken footage: lectures, tutorials, presentations, explainers, interviews and creator recordings. It edits what can be measured and asks you about what cannot.

01 / SIGNAL
◒

Evidence, not guesses

FFmpeg and FFprobe provide measurable audio and visual evidence before editorial rules make a decision.

02 / TIMELINE
⌁

One synchronized timeline

Audio and video share the same edit decisions, so tightening footage never silently breaks sync.

03 / CONTROL
◇

Conservative by design

Weak footage can be tightened aggressively. Ambiguous moments become review items instead of irreversible cuts.
